## David's Digital Hub
Welcome to the repository for my Digital Hub, a personal portfolio website built to showcase academic achievements, hands-on projects, extracurricular activities, and media tracking. This project is both my personal website and my digital portfolio! As of October 16th, only the homepage is available, other pages will be worked on as time passes!

## Project Overview
This website is completely custom, using zero AI code, it showcases:

* Academics & School Work: Tracks formal high school transcripts, coursework details, and the transition from an International Baccalaureate (IB) system at Liceo de Apodaca in Mexico to Northville High School in Michigan, USA.
* Clubs & Extracurriculars: Documents participation in engineering and community groups, including FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) Team 548 (Robostangs), the Northville High School Aerospace Club, and volunteer service at Maybury Farm.
* Certifications: Highlights technical, industry-aligned credentials pursued independently and through the Mechatronics program at the William D. Ford Technology & Career Center.
* Projects: Displays a gallery of independent, hands-on DIY hobbies, woodworking, civil volunteer projects, and upcoming robotic builds.
* Media Tracking: Serves as a digital library cataloging all-time favorite books, movies, and automated weekly music listening metrics.

## Repository Structure

* /index.html - The main landing page and introduction to the portfolio, it serves to introduce me and the thematic of the website
* /about.html - A landing page with only two flashcards, one to go to Certifications, and one to go to Education.
* /edu.html - A page with all my education, each school year being it's own flashcard, a little carousel at the end showing each extracurricular I've been into, each one with a button to "Learn More" that sends the user to a clubs.html.
* /clubs.html - A page with my extracurriculars, roles, and learning experiences.
* /certs.html - Professional badges and industry certifications.
* /projects.html - Projects I've made, may they be software, hardware or crafts, each with their own description
* /tracking.html - A little library of books I've read,movies I've watched, music genres I like and weekly music metrics.
